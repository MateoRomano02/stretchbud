# 08 - Adversarial verification of claims (2026-10-13)

Method: primary abstracts/full text via Europe PMC REST (PMC full text for Dunstan and WHO/Bull 2020, Balban), GitHub API, npm, Adobe FAQ, Columbia press page. Verdicts: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / UNVERIFIED.

## Summary of CORRECTED items
1. Dunstan 2012 insulin: the [M] figure "-26% moderate" is wrong. Paper: insulin iAUC lowered 23% (both conditions). Glucose 24-30%. Moderate walking speed was 5.8-6.4 km/h (not "~5.8").
2. Galinsky 2007: n completing = 51 (21 stretch, 30 no stretch), not 61. Supplementary breaks = four 5-min breaks per day (file implies "about every hour"; paper says only 4 per day). Data-entry speed was significantly FASTER with supplementary breaks.
3. McLean 2001: the [M] protocol guess ("30-s break every 20 min") is not in the abstract. Actual: 3 groups, microbreaks at own discretion / every 20 min / every 40 min vs no breaks; outcome is EMG (MNF cycling) + discomfort + productivity. Do not state a break length for it.
4. Andersen 2012: DASH disability fell only in 1x60 and 3x20 groups, NOT 9x7. Neck pain reductions (baseline 3.2): 1.14 / 1.88 / 1.35 for 1x60 / 3x20 / 9x7 (file's 1.14-1.88 range is right). Subsample pain>=3 was n=256 of 447 (447 included workers with and without pain).
5. Shrestha 2018: "short breaks reduce sitting ~40 min/day" is ONE study vs two 15-min long breaks (low quality). Computer prompts: no significant effect at short term (-14 min), -55 min/day only at medium term from ONE study. Sit-stand desks -100 min/day (95% CI -116 to -84, 10 studies, low quality) short term (<=3 months); -57 min medium term from 2 studies.
6. Adamczyk & Bailey 2004 (03 file): paper's abstract says best moment = 56% less annoyance than worst, 43% less than random; results section says annoyance "rose 56% from best to worst" (internally inconsistent direction; 56% reduction from worst is the abstract's wording). The "31-106%" range and "half the anxiety increase" could not be found in the paper text I extracted: UNVERIFIED, remove from README.
7. Balban 2023 (03 file): randomized group sizes in Methods are 24 mindfulness / 30 cyclic sighing / 21 box / 33 hyperventilation (= 108). The file's 22/27/19/32 (=100) may be analysed completers; not verified. Daily PANAS change: cyclic sighing 1.89 +/- 3.76 (p=.025), mindfulness 1.22 +/- 2.34 (p=.06): CONFIRMED.
8. Mark 2008 CHI (03 file): file correctly reports 22.77 vs ~20.3 min and stress; CONFIRMED. The popular "23 min 15 s to resume" figure is NOT from this paper (it is from Mark, Gonzalez, Harris, CHI 2005 field study; not fetched here). Do not attribute it to Mark 2008.
9. WHO "every move counts": that phrase is a WHO campaign slogan; it does NOT appear in the Bull 2020 BJSM text. Guideline wording is "some physical activity is better than none" and "Replacing sedentary time with physical activity of any intensity (including light intensity) provides health benefits."

## Claim-by-claim
1. Albulescu 2022 PLOS ONE: CONFIRMED. Abstract: 19 records, 22 independent samples, N=2335; vigor d=.36 (k=9, n=913); fatigue d=.35 (k=9, n=803); performance d=.16, p=.116 (k=15, n=1132) n.s.; "the longer the break, the greater the boost on performance" (meta-regression, between-study). Breaks <=10 min. Significant effects only for less cognitively demanding tasks. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0272460
2. Dunstan 2012 Diabetes Care: CORRECTED (see 1). n=19 overweight/obese 45-65 y; 2-min bouts every 20 min; light 3.2 km/h, moderate 5.8-6.4 km/h. Glucose iAUC 6.9 (sit) -> 5.2 light, 4.9 moderate (full text: "24-30% lowering"); insulin 828.6 -> 633.6 / 637.6 pmol/L.h ("23% lowering"). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3329818/
3. Diaz 2023 MSSE: CONFIRMED. n=11; 5 min every 30 min was the only dose with significantly attenuated glucose iAUC (-11.8 [4.7], p=.017); Columbia press: "reducing blood sugar spikes by 58% compared with sitting all day" (press, not abstract); all doses lowered SBP, largest 1 min/60 min (-5.2 mmHg) and 5 min/30 min (-4.3 mmHg); press says 4-5 mmHg for all. https://www.cuimc.columbia.edu/node/25711 ; DOI 10.1249/mss.0000000000003109
4. Buffey 2022 Sports Med: CONFIRMED. 7 acute crossover trials; walking vs standing: glucose -0.30 (-0.52,-0.08), insulin -0.54 (-0.75,-0.33); neither affects SBP (3 studies); effect sizes are Cohen's d-type, not mmol/L. Standing vs sitting glucose -0.31.
5. McLean 2001: CORRECTED (see 3); "particularly at 20 min intervals" and "no detrimental effect on productivity" CONFIRMED. Galinsky 2007: CORRECTED (see 2); 5-min supplementary breaks reduced discomfort/eyestrain, output maintained. Stretching compliance 25%/39% CONFIRMED.
6. Shrestha 2018 Cochrane: CORRECTED/nuanced (see 5). 34 studies, 3,397 participants CONFIRMED.
7. Andersen 2011 Pain: CONFIRMED. 174 women + 24 men, frequent neck/shoulder pain, 2 or 12 min/day x5/week x10 weeks; pain -1.4 (2 min) and -1.9 (12 min) points vs control; tenderness -4.2/-4.4. Population is people WITH frequent pain. Andersen 2012 BJSM: n=447 CONFIRMED; CORRECTED detail (see 4).
8. WHO 2020: CONFIRMED except slogan. 150-300 min moderate or 75-150 vigorous or combination; muscle-strengthening at moderate or greater intensity on 2 or more days/week; "evidence was insufficient to quantify a sedentary behaviour threshold"; replace sedentary time with activity of any intensity. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719906/
9. EFSA 2010: CONFIRMED (secondary index of the opinion): AI 2.5 L/day males, 2.0 L/day females, total water from drinking water, all beverages and food moisture; moderate temperature, PAL 1.6. The "food ~20%" share: UNVERIFIED in this session (do not print a number). https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/1459
10. Mixamo: CONFIRMED. Adobe FAQ: "really the only thing you can't do is distribute the raw character and animation files", incl. "any type of free distribution of character or animation raw files". https://community.adobe.com/t5/mixamo-discussions/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos/m-p/13234775 . RobotExpressive: CONFIRMED CC0 1.0 (README; model by Tomas Laulhe/Quaternius, modified by Don McCurdy). mannequin.js: CONFIRMED GPL-3.0 (GitHub API; last push 2025-10-05). Ready Player Me: CONFIRMED shut down 2026-01-31 (Netflix acquired 2025-12-19) per Genies/Avatar SDK posts. three.js: CONFIRMED `npm view three` latest = 0.186.1.

Competitors: CONFIRMED via GitHub API: BotchetDig/workout-gate 221 stars, MIT, created 2026-06-12; surya-krishna/fitgate 5 stars, AGPL-3.0, created 2026-09-09; Eliasjunit/vibestretch 2 stars, MIT, created 2026-07-31 (pushed 2026-10-06, so file's "updated 2026-08-21" is stale). Balban 2023 Cell Rep Med: CONFIRMED qualitatively. 5-min daily x 28 days, remote RCT; cyclic sighing gave greater mood improvement (p<0.05) and reduction in respiratory rate (p<0.05) vs mindfulness meditation. Not shown: HRV change, single-session effect, 60 s dose. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9873947/
